**For:** the three owners/partners

either gets omitted or written vaguely — and vague copy is what makes villa websites
forgettable.

**How to fill this in:** don't write marketing language. Write plain facts and honest
opinions. The copywriting is handled afterwards. If you disagree with each other on
something, write all three answers — disagreements are usually where the interesting
material is.

## 12. The story

These are the questions that produce the copy nobody else can copy. Answer them in your
own words — length doesn't matter, honesty does.

1. **How did you end up in Ekas?**

2. **What made you choose this specific piece of land?**

3. **What's the single best moment of a day at Villa 25?** *(A time of day, a spot, a view,
   a sound — be specific.)*

4. **What do people say when they arrive and see it for the first time?**

5. **What was the hardest part of building it?**

6. **What do you want guests to feel that they don't get at a hotel in Kuta or Bali?**

7. **What's changing in Ekas? What will be different in five years?**

8. **Is there anything about the place you'd want a guest to know *before* they book, so
   they're not disappointed?** *(Road, power cuts, mosquitoes, remoteness — naming these
   honestly builds far more trust than hiding them.)*
